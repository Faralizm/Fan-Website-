About La Casa de Papel (Money Heist)

La Casa de Papel (Money Heist) is a Spanish television series created by Álex Pina. The show follows a group of robbers, led by the Professor, as they plan and execute heists on the Royal Mint of Spain and the Bank of Spain. Known for its thrilling plot, complex characters, and iconic red jumpsuits and Dalí masks, the series has gained a massive global following.
